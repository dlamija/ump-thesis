\subsection{Learning Phase}
\lipsum[14] The learning process minimises the cross-entropy loss
$L$ over the training samples:
\addsymbol{$L$}{Cross-entropy loss function}

\begin{equation}
L = -\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} y_{i}\log\hat{y}_{i}
\label{eq:example}
\end{equation}

where $y_{i}$ is the true label and $\hat{y}_{i}$ is the predicted
label of sample $i$.
\addsymbol{$y_{i}$}{True label of the $i$-th training sample}
\addsymbol{$\hat{y}_{i}$}{Predicted label of the $i$-th training sample}
